# Nike Retail Sales & Profit Analysis
## Project Overview
**Project Overview**:The objective of this project is to analyze the Nike sales dataset in India to understand overall sales performance and profitability. The analysis also identifies the regions with the highest sales, examines the impact of discounts and sales channels (online and offline), and evaluates profit trends on a yearly basis.
### 📊 Dataset Description
The dataset contains **2500** rows and **15** columns

Column Name	           |         Description
|----------------------|----------------------------------------------
|Order_ID              |      Unique identifier for each sales transaction 
| Gender_Category      |      Target gender of the product 
| Product_Line         |      Category of Nike product 
| Product_Name         |      Name of the Nike product 
| Size                 |      Product size 
| Units_Sold           |      Number of units sold 
| MRP                  |      Maximum Retail Price 
| Discount_Applied     |      Discount applied to product 
| Revenue              |      Sales revenue after discount 
| Order_Date           |      Date of order 
| Sales_Channel        |      Sales platform 
| Region               |      Sales region in India 
| Profit               |      Profit from the sale 
## Key Findings
- Sales showed a noticeable increase starting from April 2024.
- The majority of customers preferred the retail sales channel over other channels.
- Sales were evenly distributed across all regions, indicating balanced performance geographically.
